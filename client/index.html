Viva / Portfolio Questions & Answers

  • Q: Why Sublinear TF-IDF? Normal term frequency favors long corporate emails. Sublinear scaling (1 + ln(count)) normalizes term frequencies so long emails and short SMS texts are judged fairly.
  • Q: How does Zero-Python serving work? Scikit-learn exported the vocabulary, IDF table, and model weights to JSON. Node.js evaluates vectorization and sigmoid inference natively in pure JavaScript in < 1 millisecond.
  • Q: How is word explainability computed? Each word's contribution is calculated as (Normalized TF-IDF × Model Coefficient). Positive values push toward SPAM, negative toward HAM.
